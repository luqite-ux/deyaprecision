# Motion Plan

- Hero: restrained cross-fade with manual controls, pause, touch and keyboard support; no autoplay-only access.
- Product and application cards: use the Anli `original-cards-motion` sequencing pattern, triggered independently when each section enters the viewport.
- Certificates: use the Anli `original-certificate-motion` rail pattern with full portrait documents, pause, previous/next and touch support.
- Capability metrics and equipment groups: short staggered reveal tied to the actual viewport; never pre-complete unreached sections with a global timeout.
- Navigation: hide only after meaningful downward scrolling, reopen on upward scroll/focus, and keep mobile navigation explicit.
- Reduced motion: disable translation/auto-advance and present static, fully visible content.
- Performance: transform/opacity only for entrance effects; images retain responsive sizing and no effect may crop product subjects.
