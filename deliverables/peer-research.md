# Overseas Peer Research

Reviewed on 2026-11-06.

## Precise Components & Tool Design

URL: https://www.precisecomponent.com/

- Reviewed homepage, navigation and capability/product sections.
- Useful: direct buyer segmentation, separate capability/equipment/quality/portfolio routes, concise proof near the first CTA, and material/application detail that helps qualification.
- Do not copy: its medical-only positioning, US origin claims, certifications, tolerance figures, traceability claims, comparison table or visual identity.
- DEYA adaptation: retain the explicit route hierarchy and evidence-first sequence, but use only DEYA’s supplied four application sectors, equipment inventory and ISO record.

## WEKO Liquid-cooling Components

URL: https://wekooem.com/capabilities/liquid-cooling-components

- Reviewed the liquid-cooling capability page, component-family navigation, interfaces/materials/inspection sequence and certificate section.
- Useful: organise a manifold buyer journey around geometry, interfaces, material, inspection scope and drawing-based RFQ; display each certificate separately and let users open the full document.
- Do not copy: WEKO wording, 3D interaction, factory claims, tests, certificate types, production route details or visual assets.
- DEYA adaptation: use the same information logic for DEYA manifolds and connectors, but restrict content to supplied dimensions, materials, application context and verified inspection resources.

## Sanmina Precision Machining

URL: https://www.sanmina.com/contract-manufacturing-design/machining/

- Reviewed the precision-machining page, hero topics, capability sequence and case-study entry structure.
- Useful: separate equipment/process capability from application case examples; present manufacturing breadth through a clear visual sequence instead of repeating generic benefit cards.
- Do not copy: global footprint, 40-year claim, 5-axis claims, statistical process-control claims, cleanroom/RGA capabilities, customers, project outcomes or imagery.
- DEYA adaptation: pair the supplied production equipment list with a separate inspection inventory and keep application examples neutral unless a real client/project is documented.

## Chosen blueprint

Use an evidence-led industrial editorial structure: product fit → machining and inspection capability → application/case context → separate certificate documents → drawing-based enquiry. The visual expression remains DEYA-specific, driven by the supplied blue logo and three customer Banner assets.
